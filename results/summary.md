## Gripper notes

Warnings from the calibration checks (tools/calibrate_grippers.py).

- **FRANKA**: no physics material on the gripper: contacts use PhysX's default friction, and the low_friction condition scales only the object's side, so it is milder than for other grippers
- **RG2**: measured opening 97.7 mm differs from the datasheet's 110.0 mm by more than 10%
- **2FG7**: pads never meet (smallest gap 6.6 mm); a grasp that closes fully is counted as missed only near the closed limit
